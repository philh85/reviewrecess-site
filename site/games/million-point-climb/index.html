Million Point Climb: a climb-the-ladder quiz show review game for your classroom

Million Point Climb is a multiple-choice quiz show for the front of the room, like the TV quiz shows students know. One contestant at a time climbs a points ladder, with lifelines, safety nets and the option to walk away.

Students answer out loud, so no student devices are needed. It suits Grades 4–12 in any subject, and the questions can get harder as the ladder climbs.

Step by step

How to play Million Point Climb

Set up the game

  1. Choose how to run it. Basic uses one screen: the question fills the screen, and a slim teacher bar sits along the bottom. Presenter (full version) gives you a clean Display for the TV and a private Controller on your laptop (or phone) that shows you the answer.
  2. Highlight one or more question sets and add them to the session. Each question has four choices, and can also have a difficulty, a category, a hint and an explanation.
  3. Check the class session settings: the prize ladder, the prize prefix, the safety-net questions and the lifelines. Add your students, one name per line.
  4. Press Start new class session.

Play a round

  1. Choose the next contestant. Each student plays one climb per class session.
  2. Read the question. The contestant picks an answer, and you select it.
  3. Press the main button to step through Lock in, then Reveal, then Next question. It's one button, so you can't press the wrong one.
  4. The contestant can use each lifeline once: 50:50, Ask the Audience, Phone a Friend, Switch the question and Ask the Host.
  5. The contestant can walk away with their points, or keep climbing.

Scoring and winning

  1. Each correct answer moves the contestant up the ladder. The ladder shows the current points and the guaranteed amount.
  2. Safety-net questions (questions 5 and 10 by default) lock in points, so a wrong answer later drops the contestant back to the last safety net.
  3. A results table records each contestant's name, points and outcome for the session.

Make it fit your class

Million Point Climb modes, settings and options

Basic: one screen

Best for one laptop on the projector. The show fills the screen, with a slim teacher bar and keyboard shortcuts.

Presenter: Display + ControllerFull version

Open the Display on the TV or an extended screen. Your Controller is the only screen that shows the correct answer.

Phone remoteFull version

In Presenter mode, open the same game link on your phone, signed in to the same Google account, and enter the 4-digit code from the Display. Expect a 1–3 second delay per tap, and keep the laptop window open.

Fastest Finger First

An optional ordering round to choose the next contestant. Students write the order on paper, and the game shows the correct order.

Prize ladder

Your own list of 5–30 rising values, with a prize prefix ($ by default).

Safety nets

Choose which question numbers lock in points.

Lifelines

Ask the Audience is simulated on screen. Phone a Friend can be a real classmate or a simulated friend, with 30 seconds. Ask the Host uses each question's hint and can be turned off.

Increasing difficulty

Questions get harder as the ladder climbs, using the difficulty levels in your question sets.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Have the class answer on mini whiteboards while the contestant thinks, so everyone works on every question.
  • Use the real-classmate option for Phone a Friend, so a second student gets to explain their thinking.
  • Add a short explanation to each question and read it out after Reveal to turn mistakes into teaching moments.
  • Play a short Fastest Finger First round to pick contestants fairly.

A Google account

The full version runs in your own Google account and saves your question banks in a Google Sheet in your Drive. A personal account works if your school blocks the games.

A computer with an up-to-date browser

Chrome works best. There's nothing to install, no copy to make and no script editor to open.

A projector, smart TV or interactive board

Free version

Questions

Million Point Climb FAQ

Do students need devices?

No. Students answer out loud, and the whole game runs on your screen.

Is Presenter mode in the free version?

No. The free version runs in Basic mode on one screen. Presenter mode, with the TV Display, the private Controller and the phone remote, is full version only.

How do I show the questions on the TV but keep the answer on my laptop?

Use Presenter mode, which is in the full version. Choose Presenter, press Open Display and drag that window to the TV or extended screen. Your laptop becomes the Controller, the only screen that shows the correct answer.

Can I use my phone as a remote?

Yes, with the full version, in Presenter mode. Open the game link on your phone, signed in to the same Google account as the laptop, and enter the 4-digit code from the Display. Each tap takes about 1–3 seconds to reach the Display.

Do questions repeat?

Not within a class session until the questions are reshuffled. Starting a new class session clears the history and lets every student play again.

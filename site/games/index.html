Quiz board

Brain Board Blitz

A 5 × 5 quiz board for teams, with 1–5 rounds, a timer, skip limits and saved games.

Teams choose a category and a point value, then answer against the clock. Save the game and pick it up next lesson.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · Any subject · Test review
Players
2+ teams

How to play Brain Board Blitz

Quiz show

Million Point Climb

Climb the points ladder with lifelines, safety nets and a Fastest Finger round.

Run it on one screen, or put a clean Display on the TV and keep the answers on your laptop or phone. Questions can get harder as the ladder rises.

Best for
Grades 4–12 · Any subject
Players
One contestant at a time

How to play Million Point Climb

Speaking game

Forbidden Words

Explain the key term without saying the forbidden words.

Teams take turns while the class listens for forbidden words. A timer, pause, undo and a scoreboard keep turns quick and fair, and the game suggests who comes up next.

Best for
Grades 5–12 · Vocabulary in any subject
Players
2–10 teams or 2–20 pairs

How to play Forbidden Words

Team quiz

Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game

Answer a question to claim a square. Three in a row wins the round.

Two teams take turns in player order, so everyone answers, not just the fastest hands. Steal Mode gives the other team a chance at a missed question.

Best for
Grades 2–12 · Any subject
Players
2 teams: X vs O

How to play Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game

Word game

Letter Lifeline

Guess the letters before the picture runs out, with an optional clue under every word.

Use it for spelling lists or unit vocabulary. Pick one of 20 friendly picture themes, or the Skeleton style for Grades 7–12.

Best for
Grades 1–12 · Spelling & vocabulary
Players
1 vs 1 · teams · whole class

How to play Letter Lifeline

Whole class vs teacher

Review Recess Answer Armada

The class answers questions to earn shots and hunt for the teacher's hidden ships.

Every correct answer earns exactly one shot, which keeps the whole room working toward one goal. Three difficulty levels, plus five picture styles for K–6.

Best for
Grades 2–12 · Any subject
Players
Whole class vs teacher

How to play Review Recess Answer Armada

Parts-of-speech puzzle

Mystery Cat

Guess letters to reveal the hidden words in a mystery sentence.

Teams take turns guessing letters to fill the blanks. Each bank focuses on a part of speech, with an optional bonus for naming it.

Best for
Grades 2–9 · Grammar & vocabulary
Players
2–6 teams

How to play Mystery Cat

Speaking warm-up

Pass the Bomb

Answer and pass before the hidden timer runs out.

A fast, hot-potato style warm-up. Choose a bomb, a jack-in-the-box, a sleeping bear or a sleeping baby.

Best for
Grades K–12 · Speaking · Warm-ups
Players
Whole class · circle game

How to play Pass the Bomb

Speaking race

5-Second Battle

Teams race the clock to answer prompts aloud.

Works for any topic and any language, with English review, English ESL and Spanish samples. Steals and model answers keep every team listening.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · Review · ESL & Spanish
Players
2–6 teams

How to play 5-Second Battle

Sentence building

Sentence Slot Machine

Spin up to five reels and build a sentence before time runs out.

Each reel holds words or phrases you choose. Students combine the results into one sentence, out loud.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · ELA, ESL & world languages
Players
2 teams

How to play Sentence Slot Machine

Drag-and-drop grammar

Review Recess Word Magnets

Drag the gold word onto its partner: adverbs, adjectives, subjects, objects, pronouns and phrases.

Six grammar modes plus Mixed, with magnet tiles that snap together on a right answer. Formerly “What Does It Modify?”.

Best for
Grades 3–9 · ELA grammar
Players
Whole class · random students

How to play Review Recess Word Magnets

Unscramble sentences

Review Recess Sentence Snap

Drag the scrambled word tiles into order to build the sentence.

Correct tiles lock in green. Make the end mark a tile, hide the capital letter, or add a timer for a challenge.

Best for
Grades 2–8 · ELA
Players
Whole class or 2–6 teams

How to play Review Recess Sentence Snap

Unscramble words

Word Scramble

Drag the mixed-up letters into order to spell the word.

Each word can have a clue or definition. Shuffle again or use a hint when students are stuck.

Best for
Grades 2–8 · Spelling & vocabulary
Players
Whole class or 2–6 teams

How to play Word Scramble

Included in every game

Saved question banks

Load, edit, save and reuse banks, or paste rows straight from a spreadsheet.

Question picker

Select all, or untick individual questions or whole categories before you start.

Team rosters

Save each class once and use a fair student picker during play.

Automatic reshuffle

No question repeats until all have been used.

Display options

Light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen.

Your Drive, your data

Questions and rosters are saved in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive.

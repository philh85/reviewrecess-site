Quiz board

Brain Board Blitz

A 5 × 5 quiz board for teams, with a timer, scores and saved games.

Teams choose a category and a points value, then answer against the clock. Save the game and pick it up next lesson, or start a fresh round with unused questions.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · Any subject · Test review
Players
2+ teams
Quiz show

Million Point Climb

Climb the points ladder with lifelines, safety nets and a Fastest Finger round.

A one-screen quiz show for the front of the room. Questions get harder as the ladder rises, and difficulty levels can be mixed from your own question sets.

Best for
Grades 4–12 · Any subject
Players
Students or teams
Speaking game

Forbidden Words

Explain the key term without saying the forbidden words.

Teams take turns while the class listens for forbidden words. A timer, pause, undo and a team scoreboard keep turns quick and fair, and the game calls students up in rotation.

Best for
Grades 5–12 · Vocabulary in any subject
Players
2+ teams
Team quiz

Quiz Tac Toe

Answer a question to claim a square. Three in a row wins the round.

Two teams take turns in player order, so everyone answers, not just the fastest hands. Steal mode gives the other team a chance at a missed question.

Best for
Grades 2–12 · Any subject
Players
2 teams: X vs O
Word game

Letter Lifeline

Guess the letters before the picture runs out, with an optional clue under every word.

Use it for spelling lists or unit vocabulary. Pick one of 20 friendly picture themes or let it surprise you each word, and the clue line turns guessing into a recall exercise.

Best for
Grades 1–10 · Spelling & vocabulary
Players
1 vs 1 · teams · whole class
Whole class vs teacher

Grammar Battleships

The class answers questions to earn shots at the teacher's fleet.

An optional question card sits beside the grid. Every correct answer earns a shot, which keeps the whole room working toward one goal.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · Grammar · Any subject
Players
Whole class vs teacher
Phrase puzzle

Mystery Cat

Guess letters to reveal the hidden words in a mystery phrase.

Teams take turns guessing letters to fill the blanks. Write phrases around your unit's vocabulary and descriptive language.

Best for
Grades 2–9 · Vocabulary · Facts
Players
2–6 teams
Speaking warm-up

Pass the Bomb

Answer and pass before the hidden timer runs out.

A fast, hot-potato style warm-up. The timer is hidden, so students keep answers short and keep the prompt moving.

Best for
Grades 2–12 · Speaking · Warm-ups
Players
Whole class · circle game
Speaking race

5-Second Battle

Two teams race the clock to answer prompts aloud.

Works for any topic and any language. Comes with English review, English ESL and Spanish samples. Steals and model answers keep both teams listening.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · Review · ESL & Spanish
Players
2 teams
Sentence building

Sentence Slot Machine

Spin up to five reels and build a sentence before time runs out.

Each reel holds words or phrases you choose. Students combine the results into a sentence, which makes it a quick way to practice sentence structures.

Best for
Grades 3–12 · ESL & world languages
Players
2 teams
Grammar challenge

What Does It Modify?

Click the adverb, then the word it modifies.

A focused grammar game with streaks and a class score. A random student is called for each sentence.

Best for
Grades 3–9 · ELA grammar
Players
Whole class · random students

Included in every game

Question picker

Display options

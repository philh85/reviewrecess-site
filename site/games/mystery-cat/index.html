Mystery Cat: a parts-of-speech letter-guessing puzzle for teams

In Mystery Cat, a sentence appears with some of its words hidden. Teams take turns guessing letters to reveal them, then try to solve the words and finally the whole sentence. A detective cat reacts to every guess.

Each bank focuses on one part of speech (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions or conjunctions) or on general vocabulary, so it's a grammar game and a vocabulary game in one. It suits Grades 2–9 with 2–6 teams.

Step by step

How to play Mystery Cat

Set up the game

  1. Choose a phrase bank. Each bank has a word type, and all the wording on screen follows it, for example "Guess a verb".
  2. Turn the "Name the part of speech" bonus on or off.
  3. Choose the number of teams (2–6) and type each team's name and members, or load the teams from a roster. Press START GAME.

Play a round

  1. The sentence appears with the hidden words as blanks. The turn display shows the team and the player.
  2. The player guesses a letter by clicking it or typing it. Every matching letter is revealed.
  3. On its turn, a team can press 💬 Guess a word (the label follows the word type). You see the answer and mark it ✓ Correct or ✗ Incorrect.
  4. When 3 or fewer hidden letters are left, the final challenge starts: guess the whole phrase.
  5. Press NEXT CAT ➜ for the next sentence.

Scoring and winning

  1. A letter that's in the phrase is +1, and a letter that isn't is −1.
  2. A correct word is +2, and an incorrect word is −1.
  3. In the final challenge, the whole phrase is +5. A wrong guess is −2 and passes to the next team.
  4. With the bonus on, the team that solves a word can win +1 more for naming its part of speech.

Make it fit your class

Mystery Cat modes, settings and options

Word type per bank

Adjectives, nouns, verbs, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions or vocabulary.

Mixed parts of speech

Tag words like {quickly|adverb}, so one sentence can hide several types of word.

Name the part of speech bonus

Off by default. After a word is solved, show the answer and give +1 or no bonus. Solved words get a colored tag.

Phrase banks

Hide words by putting them in {braces}. It comes with 8 sample banks for Grades 2–6: one per part of speech, plus a mixed bank.

Teams

2–6 teams, with players who rotate within each team.

Fits on one screen

Everything is visible without scrolling, even with 6 teams on a 1280 × 720 screen.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Before each new sentence, ask the class which part of speech is hidden and why.
  • Use the bonus to check understanding: students must name the part of speech, not just spell the word.
  • Write sentences from your current class novel or unit, so grammar practice reinforces content.
  • Use ↺ Restart cat (scores are kept) if a sentence was too hard.

Ideas by subject

Ways to use it across the curriculum

ELA

Adjectives in descriptive writing, strong verbs, adverbs of manner, conjunctions in compound sentences.

Science

Facts with hidden key terms: Plants make food through {photosynthesis}.

Social studies

Facts with hidden names and places from the unit.

Math

Math vocabulary hidden in definitions: A {triangle} has three sides.

Questions

Mystery Cat FAQ

Is it only for adjectives?

No. Each bank can focus on adjectives, nouns, verbs, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions or general vocabulary, and tags let you mix word types in one sentence.

How do I hide a word?

Put it in braces, for example: The {fluffy} cat slept. To mark its part of speech in a mixed bank, write {quickly|adverb}.

Do students need to scroll?

No. The play screen fits on one screen, even with 6 teams.

Can students type their letter guesses?

Yes. Letters can be clicked on screen or typed on the keyboard.

Sentence Slot Machine: spin the reels and build a sentence out loud

Sentence Slot Machine gives students a random who, what, when and where, and challenges them to say one complete sentence that uses all of it before time runs out.

You choose what goes on every reel, so it fits any grammar point or vocabulary list. It's built for two teams and suits ELA, ESL and world-language classes in Grades 3–12.

Step by step

How to play Sentence Slot Machine

Set up the game

  1. Type the two team names, or load them from a roster.
  2. Choose the speaking time (3 to 60 seconds) and whether the timer starts automatically after the spin.
  3. Fill the reels. There are up to 5 reels, each with a title and one item per line. Turn reels on or off. You need at least 2.
  4. Press ▶ START GAME.

Play a round

  1. Press 🎰 SPIN! The reels stop one by one.
  2. The team says a sentence out loud that uses every result. The timer starts after the spin, or when you press ⏱ START TIMER.
  3. Press ✓ CORRECT or ✗ MISS. Then it's the other team's turn.

Scoring and winning

  1. A correct sentence is +1, or +2 if a DOUBLE POINTS item came up.
  2. A miss scores nothing.
  3. Use − / + to adjust the scores, and 🏁 End game for the final scores.

Make it fit your class

Sentence Slot Machine modes, settings and options

Up to 5 reels

For example WHO?, WHAT?, WHEN?, WHERE? and a CHAOS reel.

Chaos items

Items like ⚡ 3 SECONDS change that turn's timer, and 💣 DOUBLE POINTS doubles the score.

Automatic or teacher-started timer

Start the countdown right after the spin, or give students thinking time first.

Speaking time

3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds.

Reel editor

See the reels side by side with item counts, reorder them, add up to 5, or paste a column from a spreadsheet.

Samples

English: Who? What? When? Where?, and English ESL: Daily routines.

Keyboard

Space spins, C marks correct, X marks a miss and T starts the timer.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Set a grammar target before you spin, such as the past tense, a compound sentence or a question.
  • Give English learners 30–60 seconds and a sentence frame on the board.
  • Turn on the CHAOS reel once students are confident.
  • Ask the other team to repeat or improve the sentence for a bonus point.

Ideas by subject

Ways to use it across the curriculum

ELA

Subjects, verbs and time phrases to practice tenses and complete sentences.

World languages

Reels in Spanish or French to practice conjugation and word order.

Science

Animals, habitats and adaptations to build science sentences.

Social studies

People, actions and places from the unit to retell history.

Questions

Sentence Slot Machine FAQ

How many reels can I use?

Up to 5, and at least 2. Each reel can be turned on or off.

Can I make the timer longer?

Yes. Choose up to 60 seconds of speaking time.

Can I use it for Spanish or French?

Yes. The reels can hold any words or phrases in any language.

How many teams can play?

Two teams take turns.

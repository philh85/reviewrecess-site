Word Scramble: a drag-the-letters unscramble game for spelling and vocabulary

Word Scramble shows a word as mixed-up letter tiles, with an optional clue or definition. Students drag the letters into order and press Check. Letters in the right place lock in green.

Use it for spelling lists and content vocabulary in Grades 2–8. Play as a whole class or with 2–6 teams, with an optional timer.

Step by step

How to play Word Scramble

Set up the game

  1. Choose a word bank, and use ☑ Choose… to pick the words.
  2. Choose the game mode: Whole class or Teams (2–6 teams, or load a class roster).
  3. Set the words per game (5, 10, 15, 20 or all), the timer, clues, the category, the hint button, and lowercase or UPPERCASE letters.
  4. Press ▶ Start game.

Play a round

  1. The clue appears, with the scrambled letter tiles below it.
  2. Students drag each letter into a slot, or tap a letter and then a slot.
  3. Press ✓ Check. Letters in the right place lock in green, and wrong ones get a red ✕.
  4. Stuck? 🔀 Shuffle again mixes the remaining letters, and 💡 Hint shows the first wrong letter. Then press Next ▶.

Scoring and winning

  1. In Teams mode, a first-try solve is 2 points, and a solve after a retry or a hint is 1 point. Use − / + to adjust.
  2. In Whole class mode, the class counts how many words it solves.
  3. 👁 Reveal shows the word with no points. 🏁 End game shows the podium.

Make it fit your class

Word Scramble modes, settings and options

Whole class or teams

Solve together, or let 2–6 teams take turns.

Clues on or off

Show the definition as a clue, or hide it for a pure spelling challenge.

Other accepted answers

A word can accept other correct answers, such as listen and silent.

Timer

Off, 30 seconds, 60 seconds, 90 seconds or 2 minutes.

Letters

Lowercase or UPPERCASE tiles.

Categories

Show each word's category, such as Life science, as an extra clue.

Same-size tiles

Tiles stay the same size all game, in the light, dark and high-contrast themes.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Write clues as definitions, so students practice meaning as well as spelling.
  • Use UPPERCASE tiles for younger students who are still learning lowercase letters.
  • Ask students to use the word in a sentence after they solve it.
  • Turn clues off for a second round with the same list.

Questions

Word Scramble FAQ

Can I add clues or definitions?

Yes. Each word can have a clue or definition, and you can show or hide clues on the setup screen.

What if the letters make another word too?

You can add other accepted answers, so an anagram like silent for listen still counts.

Does it work on an interactive whiteboard?

Yes. Drag the letters with a finger, or tap a letter and then a slot.

Can I play without teams?

Yes. Whole class mode lets everyone solve together and counts how many words you get.

Brain Board Blitz: a game-show style quiz board for team review in any subject

Brain Board Blitz is a classic five-category quiz board for the front of the room. Teams take turns choosing a category and a point value, a student answers against an optional timer, and the scores update live.

Use it for end-of-unit and test review in any subject from Grades 3 to 12. Play one round or up to five. With the full version, you can save the game and finish it next lesson.

Step by step

How to play Brain Board Blitz

Set up the game

  1. Choose a question set and a class roster. The board needs five complete categories, each with questions at levels 1 to 5.
  2. Pick the number of rounds (1–5) and the Round 1 point values (1–5, 10–50, 100–500 or 200–1000). Each later round adds a step: Round 2 is double Round 1, Round 3 is triple, and so on.
  3. Set the skips per team (unlimited or 1–5), then press Start game. You need at least two active teams.

Play a round

  1. The team in control picks a category and a value. The question opens in a large window.
  2. Press 🎲 Pick to choose a student from that team. The picker is fair, so everyone gets a turn before anyone repeats.
  3. Start the timer if you want one (10, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds), then press 👁 Reveal answer.
  4. Mark the answer ✓ Correct, ✕ Incorrect, or Skip / no points. Used squares are marked on the board.
  5. When every square is used, press ⏭ Next round. Later rounds use questions you haven't played yet.

Scoring and winning

  1. A correct answer adds the points, and the team keeps control.
  2. An incorrect answer takes the points away, and control passes to the next team.
  3. Skip / no points uses one of that team's skips. You can still fix any score with the − / + buttons, and ↶ Undo reverses the last answer.
  4. The team with the most points after the last round wins.

Make it fit your class

Brain Board Blitz modes, settings and options

1 to 5 rounds

A quick one-round game or a longer tournament. Point values grow each round.

Skip limits

Unlimited skips, or 1–5 per team for the whole game, so teams can't skip every hard question.

Save and resumeFull version

The game saves itself after every answer. Pick it up next lesson with 📂 Resume a saved game.

Point values

1–5, 10–50, 100–500 or 200–1000 for Round 1.

Timer

Optional: 10, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds, with a sound at zero.

Question library

Load, save and delete sets, add questions by category and level, import or export CSV, or paste rows from Google Sheets. A readiness check shows any missing category or level.

Team rosters

Paste one student per line as Team, Student, and untick absent students after loading.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Write level 1 questions everyone can answer, and save level 5 for stretch questions. That gives every student a chance to score.
  • Set skips to 1 or 2 per team so teams try the harder squares instead of passing.
  • Use the timer for older classes, and turn it off for younger students or English learners.
  • With the full version, play Round 1 one day and resume the saved game the next for a two-day review.

A Google account

The full version runs in your own Google account and saves your question banks in a Google Sheet in your Drive. A personal account works if your school blocks the games.

Chrome works best. There's nothing to install, no copy to make and no script editor to open.

Questions

Brain Board Blitz FAQ

How many questions do I need?

One question for each of the 5 categories at each of the 5 levels (25 in all) for one round. For more rounds, add more per category and level. The readiness check shows exactly what's missing.

Can I stop and finish the game next lesson?

Yes, in the full version. The game saves itself after every answer, and 💾 Save saves it whenever you like. Next lesson, choose 📂 Resume a saved game. Save and resume isn't included in the free version.

Can I correct a mistake?

Yes. ↶ Undo reverses the last answer, and every team score has − / + buttons.

How many teams can play?

At least two. Teams come from your class roster, and you can untick absent students.

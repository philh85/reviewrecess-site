Review Recess Word Magnets: a drag-and-drop grammar game for parts of speech

Review Recess Word Magnets turns sentences into magnet tiles on a board. One word is a gold magnet, and students drag it onto the word it belongs with: the word an adverb modifies, the noun an adjective describes, a pronoun's antecedent and more. A right answer snaps on with a gold link.

It's a whole-class grammar game for Grades 3–9 that works with a mouse or by touch on an interactive board. It was formerly called "What Does It Modify?".

Step by step

How to play Review Recess Word Magnets

Set up the game

  1. Choose a mode: one of six grammar modes, or Mixed, where each sentence keeps its own mode. Each mode card shows how many sentences the bank has for it.
  2. Choose a sentence bank, and use ☑ Choose… to pick which sentences to use.
  3. If you like, type your class list (or load a roster) so the game can call students up.
  4. Press ▶ Start game.

Play a round

  1. A sentence appears as word tiles, with one gold magnet tile. The instruction line tells students what to find.
  2. Press 🎲 Call a student. Everyone is called once before anyone repeats.
  3. The student drags the gold magnet onto its partner word, or taps the partner word.
  4. If it's right, the magnet snaps on with a gold link and a ✓, and the feedback bar explains why. If it's wrong, the tile shakes and gets a red ✕, and the student can try again.
  5. Press Next sentence ▸. Use 👀 Reveal to show the answer, or Skip ▸ to move on.

Scoring and winning

  1. The class earns a point for each sentence solved and builds a streak for answers in a row.
  2. Streak badges appear at 3, 5, 7 and 10, with confetti every 5 in a row. A wrong answer resets the streak.
  3. Game over shows the class score, the best streak and the accuracy.

Make it fit your class

Review Recess Word Magnets modes, settings and options

Adverbs

Drag the adverb onto the verb, adjective or adverb it modifies ("What does it modify?").

Adjectives

Drag the adjective onto the noun it describes.

Subjects and objects

Drag the verb onto its subject, or onto its direct object.

Pronouns

Drag the pronoun onto its antecedent.

Prepositional phrases

The whole phrase is one magnet. Drag it onto the word it modifies.

Mixed

Each sentence keeps its own mode, for cumulative review.

Sentence banks

Each row has a sentence, the magnet word, the partner word and the mode. Samples for Grades 3–8 cover all six modes, plus a mixed review and a 40-sentence adverb bank.

Touch and keyboard

Drag with a mouse, pen or finger, tap the partner word, or use Tab and Enter.

Fixed sentence area

One font size for the whole bank, so nothing jumps between short and long sentences.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Ask the student to explain their choice out loud before they drag. The feedback bar then confirms it.
  • Start a unit with one mode, then switch to Mixed for review.
  • Have the class show thumbs up or down before the student drops the magnet.
  • Use the pronoun mode during writing lessons to talk about unclear antecedents.

Questions

Review Recess Word Magnets FAQ

Is this the old "What Does It Modify?" game?

Yes. It was renamed Review Recess Word Magnets and now has six grammar modes plus Mixed. The adverb mode still asks "What does it modify?".

Does it work on an interactive whiteboard?

Yes. Students can drag with a finger or a pen, or simply tap the partner word.

Are student names saved?

No. Names you type stay on the screen only and are never saved.

Which grammar skills does it cover?

Adverbs, adjectives, subjects, direct objects, pronouns and their antecedents, and prepositional phrases.

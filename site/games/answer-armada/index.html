Whole class vs teacher

Answer Armada: a hidden-ships review game where every right answer earns a shot

Review Recess Answer Armada turns any question set into a team mission. The whole class plays together against the teacher. A correct answer earns exactly one shot at a lettered and numbered grid, and the class has to find and sink every hidden ship before the shots run out.

It works for any subject, from early elementary to high school. Younger classes can hunt for dinosaurs, eggs or animals instead of ships, and older classes get the Classic Fleet.

Step by step

How to play Answer Armada

Set up the game

  1. Open the game and choose a picture style: Classic Fleet (Grades 7–12) or one of five K–6 styles: Sea Safari, Dino Dig, Space Explorer, Spring Egg Hunt or Jungle Safari.
  2. Pick a difficulty. Easy is a 7 × 5 grid with 5 ships and 28 shots, Normal is 7 × 5 with 3 ships and 21 shots, and Hard is a bigger 9 × 7 grid with 3 ships and 28 shots.
  3. Choose a question bank (or use the general review sample), and use ☑ Choose… to untick any questions you don't want today. You can also hide the questions and ask your own out loud.
  4. Press Start game and go full screen. The ships are placed at random every game.

Play a round

  1. Press Next question. The question appears as the largest text on the screen, and the grid stays locked (🔒) until you mark the answer.
  2. Call on a student (or use 🎤 Pick a student) to answer. Press Show answer if you want the class to see it.
  3. Press ✓ Correct to unlock the grid for exactly one shot. Press ✗ Incorrect to show the answer and move on with no shot.
  4. The class calls a square, such as C4, and you click it. The game shows a miss, a hit or a sunk ship. Extra clicks are ignored, and the next question comes up by itself with the grid locked again.

Scoring and winning

  1. A hit shows only a neutral marker, so nobody can tell which ship it was. A ship's full picture appears only when every square of it has been found.
  2. The scoreboard tracks shots left, hits with accuracy, ships still afloat and the level.
  3. If the class sinks every ship, the class wins. If the shots run out first, the teacher survives and the hidden ships are shown.

Make it fit your class

Answer Armada modes, settings and options

Classic Fleet (Grades 7–12)

Navy ships on a sea grid. This is the default style.

Five K–6 picture styles

Sea Safari, Dino Dig, Space Explorer, Spring Egg Hunt and Jungle Safari. The rules stay the same. Only the pictures, wording and sounds change.

Easy, Normal and Hard

More ships make Easy friendlier. Hard uses a bigger grid, so every shot counts.

Question banks

Use the samples (General review and Grammar review) or your own banks, and tick exactly which questions to use.

Hide questions

Hide the question card and read your own questions aloud, which is handy for quick oral review.

Sound on or off

Short sound effects for misses, hits and sunk ships. Turn them off from setup or the teacher bar.

Display options

Light, dark and high-contrast themes, bigger text and larger buttons for the projector.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Let a different student call the square each time, so the whole room is involved even when one student answers.
  • Use 🎤 Pick a student for fair turns. Everyone gets a go before anyone repeats.
  • Differentiate with difficulty: Easy for a quick win at the end of a lesson, Hard for a longer test-prep session.
  • Press Show fleet at the end and talk about which squares would have been better guesses.

Questions

Answer Armada FAQ

Can students fire more than one shot per question?

No. The grid is locked while a question is up. ✓ Correct unlocks it for exactly one square, even if students click several squares quickly, and ✗ Incorrect gives no shot. An unused shot never carries over to the next question.

Is it suitable for kindergarten and lower elementary?

Yes. Five picture styles (Sea Safari, Dino Dig, Space Explorer, Spring Egg Hunt and Jungle Safari) are made for Grades K–6. The questions are up to you, so you can keep them as simple as you need.

Can I use it without a question bank?

Yes. Press Hide questions and ask your own questions out loud. Press Next question for each one, then ✓ Correct or ✗ Incorrect as usual. A correct answer still earns exactly one shot.

How long does a game take?

It depends on the difficulty and how quickly you move through questions. Normal has 21 shots, so a game usually fits into one review session.

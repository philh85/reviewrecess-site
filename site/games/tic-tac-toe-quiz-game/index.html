Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game: answer a question to claim a square

The Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game puts a review question behind every square. Two teams, X and O, take turns choosing a square and answering. A correct answer claims it, and three in a row wins the round.

Players answer in order, so every student gets a turn, not just the fastest hands. It works for any subject in Grades 2–12.

Step by step

How to play Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game

Set up the game

  1. Type the names of Team X and Team O, and list each team's players, one per line. Players rotate in order.
  2. Load a saved question bank, or type the questions as question | answer, one per line, and save the bank.
  3. Choose the game mode: Standard (a wrong answer ends the turn) or Steal Mode (the other team gets one chance).
  4. Press START GAME.

Play a round

  1. The team whose turn it is picks a square, and a question appears.
  2. The player whose turn it is answers. Press REVEAL ANSWER.
  3. Press ✓ CORRECT to claim the square, or ✕ INCORRECT. In Steal Mode, the other team's next player then gets one chance at the same question.
  4. Play passes to the other team. Keep going until a team gets three in a row or the board is full.

Scoring and winning

  1. Three in a row wins the round, with confetti and a win sound. A full board with no winner is a draw.
  2. Each team has a rounds-won counter. Press NEXT ROUND to play again.
  3. A new round keeps the remaining questions and the player rotation.

Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game modes, settings and options

Standard

A wrong answer ends the turn.

Steal Mode

After a wrong answer, the other team's next player gets one chance to steal the square.

Teacher panel

↶ Undo last move, ⏭ Skip question, ↺ New round, sound on or off, full screen and 🏁 End game.

Question banks

Save as many banks as you like, load them next lesson, or open the question sheet to edit them all at once.

No repeats

Questions are shuffled, and none repeats until every question has been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Use Steal Mode to keep the other team listening on every question.
  • Let the team talk it over for 10 seconds, but only the player whose turn it is may answer.
  • Play best of three or best of five rounds.
  • Talk about strategy with older classes: the center square and corners matter.

Questions

Tic-Tac-Toe Quiz Game FAQ

How many students can play?

Any number, split into two teams. List each team's players, and they answer in rotation.

What is Steal Mode?

When a team answers incorrectly, the other team's next player gets one chance at the same question.

Can I fix a mistake?

Yes. ↶ Undo last move is in the teacher panel.

Do questions carry over between rounds?

Yes. A new round keeps the remaining questions and the player rotation.

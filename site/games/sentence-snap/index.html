Unscramble sentences

Review Recess Sentence Snap: a drag-to-unscramble sentence game for your classroom screen

Review Recess Sentence Snap shows a sentence as scrambled word tiles. Students drag (or tap) the tiles into order and press Check. Tiles in the right place lock in green, and wrong ones get a red ✕ so students can fix them.

It's built for sentence structure, capitalization and punctuation in Grades 2–8. Play as a whole class or with 2–6 teams.

Step by step

How to play Review Recess Sentence Snap

Set up the game

  1. Choose a sentence bank, and use ☑ Choose… to pick the sentences.
  2. Choose the game mode: Whole class or Teams (2–6 teams, or load a class roster).
  3. Set the sentences per game (5, 10, 15, 20 or all), the timer (off, 30 s, 60 s, 90 s or 2 min), and whether to show hints, the category and the hint button.
  4. Choose how the end mark works (locked at the end, or a tile along with any commas) and whether the first word's capital is shown or hidden. Press ▶ Start game.

Play a round

  1. The sentence appears as shuffled word tiles above a row of empty slots.
  2. Students drag each tile into a slot, or tap a tile and then a slot. Tapping a tile twice drops it into the next empty slot.
  3. Press ✓ Check. Correct tiles lock in green, and wrong ones get a red ✕. Fix them and check again.
  4. Stuck? Use 💡 Hint to show the first wrong tile, or 🔀 Shuffle again. Then press Next ▶.

Scoring and winning

  1. In Teams mode, a first-try solve is 2 points, and a solve after a retry or a hint is 1 point. Use − / + to adjust.
  2. In Whole class mode, the class counts how many sentences it solves.
  3. 👁 Reveal shows the answer with no points. 🏁 End game shows the podium.

Make it fit your class

Review Recess Sentence Snap modes, settings and options

Whole class or teams

Solve together, or let 2–6 teams take turns.

End mark and commas

Keep the end mark locked in place, or make it (and any commas) tiles too.

Capital letter clue

Show the first word's capital as an easier clue, or hide it for a harder game.

Timer

Off, 30 seconds, 60 seconds, 90 seconds or 2 minutes.

Hints and categories

Show or hide each sentence's hint and category, and turn the 💡 Hint button on or off.

Other correct orders

A sentence can accept more than one correct word order.

Same-size tiles

Tiles stay the same size all game, in the light, dark and high-contrast themes.

Every Review Recess game also has light, dark and high-contrast themes, big text, large buttons and full screen, plus a tick-box question picker and an automatic reshuffle so no question repeats until all have been used.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Hide the capital letter and make the end mark a tile once students are ready for a challenge.
  • Ask students to read the finished sentence aloud with expression.
  • Use sentences with prepositional phrases to show how word order changes meaning.
  • In Teams mode, give each team member a turn at the board.

Questions

Review Recess Sentence Snap FAQ

Can students play on an interactive whiteboard?

Yes. Drag the tiles with a finger, or tap a tile and then a slot.

What if a sentence has more than one correct order?

You can add other correct orders to a sentence, and any of them is accepted.

Can I make it harder?

Yes. Hide the first word's capital, make the end mark and commas into tiles, turn hints off, or add a timer.

How does team scoring work?

Two points for a first-try solve, and one point after a retry or a hint.

Speaking race

5-Second Battle: a name-three-things speaking race for 2–6 teams

5-Second Battle is a quick-fire speaking game. A team gets a prompt like "Name 3 planets" and has to answer out loud before the countdown hits zero. If they miss, the next team can steal.

It works for any topic and any language, with English review, English ESL and Spanish samples. Play with 2–6 teams in Grades 3–12.

Step by step

How to play 5-Second Battle

Set up the game

  1. Choose the number of teams (2–6) and type the team names, or load them from a roster.
  2. Set the seconds per question and the steal time (each from 3 to 30 seconds).
  3. Choose the question order (random or in order) and your question bank. Each line is a prompt and a model answer, separated by |.
  4. Press ▶ START GAME.

Play a round

  1. The prompt appears for the team whose turn it is. Questions rotate through the teams in order.
  2. Press ⚡ START TIMER. The team answers out loud before the buzzer.
  3. Press ✓ CORRECT if they did it, or 🔥 STEAL to give the next team a chance with the steal timer. Press STEAL again to pass it on until every other team has tried.
  4. Press 👀 REVEAL to show the model answer, then NEXT →.

Scoring and winning

  1. A correct answer is +1 for the team that gave it, including on a steal.
  2. Each team has − / + buttons for manual changes. Scores never go below zero.
  3. Press 🏁 End game to show the winner (or a draw) and the final scores.

Make it fit your class

5-Second Battle modes, settings and options

2–6 teams

Turns rotate through the teams automatically.

Steals

On a miss, the next team in order gets a chance, and the steal keeps passing until every team has tried.

Random or in order

Shuffle the prompts, or play them in your order.

Seconds per question

3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 seconds.

Steal time

3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 seconds.

Model answers

Add an answer after the | so you can reveal examples after each turn.

Keyboard

Space starts the timer, R reveals, C marks correct, S steals, and → or N goes to the next question.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Use 10–15 seconds for younger students and English learners, and 5 seconds for a real challenge.
  • Have each team choose a different speaker every turn so everyone answers.
  • Reveal the model answer every time. It's a quick way to teach new vocabulary.
  • Use it as a five-minute bell-ringer or an exit activity.

Questions

5-Second Battle FAQ

How many teams can play?

From 2 to 6. Questions rotate through the teams automatically.

Can I give students more time?

Yes. Choose anything from 3 to 30 seconds per question, and a separate steal time from 3 to 30 seconds.

Does it work for world languages?

Yes. Prompts can be in any language, and it comes with English ESL and Spanish samples.

Who decides if an answer is correct?

You do. Press ✓ CORRECT or 🔥 STEAL after each turn.

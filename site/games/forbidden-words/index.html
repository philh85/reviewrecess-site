Forbidden Words: a vocabulary game where students explain without the forbidden words

Forbidden Words is a speaking game for vocabulary review. A student explains the word on the card without saying any of the forbidden words underneath it, while the class tries to guess it before the timer runs out.

Because students have to explain ideas in their own words, it's great for academic vocabulary in any subject. It suits Grades 5–12 and works with 2–10 teams or 2–20 pairs.

Step by step

How to play Forbidden Words

Set up the game

  1. Choose Team mode or Pair mode.
  2. Choose a card deck. Each card is a word plus its forbidden words.
  3. Set the round length (30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 seconds), what happens when someone calls "Forbidden!", and who guesses.
  4. Enter your teams and students (or load a roster), or set up your pairs, then press ▶ Start competition.

Play a round

  1. Choose which team goes next and tap a student to come up and explain. The next student in rotation is suggested.
  2. Press Start. The student explains the word on the card without saying it or any of the "Don't say" words.
  3. Press ✔ Correct when someone guesses it, ⏭ Pass to skip the card, or 🚫 Forbidden! if a forbidden word slips out.
  4. When time runs out, the Time's up screen shows how many cards were correct, passed and forbidden. Press Next for the next team.

Scoring and winning

  1. Each correct guess is +1 for the explainer's team (or pair).
  2. "Forbidden!" follows your setting: skip the card with no penalty (the default), −1 for the explaining side, or +1 for the team that called it.
  3. ↶ Undo fixes a mis-tap. 🏆 Scoreboard shows the team scoreboard or the pair leaderboard at any time.

Make it fit your class

Forbidden Words modes, settings and options

Team mode

Call students up one at a time. The whole class guesses, and the points go to the student's team.

Pair mode

Partners come to the screen in turn. One explains and the other guesses, and they swap next time. The class watches for forbidden words.

Who guesses

The whole class (points still go to the explainer's team) or only the explainer's team.

Round length

30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 seconds.

Forbidden penalty

No penalty, −1 for the explaining side, or +1 for the team that called it.

Fixed-size card

The word, the forbidden words, the timer and the buttons stay in the same place whether a card has one line or two.

Saved competition

The competition is saved in the browser, so you can resume it next lesson.

From the classroom

Tips for teachers

  • Model one card yourself first, so students hear what a good explanation sounds like.
  • For younger students or English learners, use fewer forbidden words and a longer round.
  • Make one student per team the forbidden-word judge to keep the whole class listening.
  • Afterward, ask students to write a definition for any card that was passed.

Ideas by subject

Ways to use it across the curriculum

Science

Photosynthesis, erosion, gravity or ecosystem, with forbidden words like plant, sun or water.

Social studies

Democracy, treaty, migration, amendment.

ELA

Literary terms such as metaphor, protagonist and theme.

Math

Perimeter, denominator, parallel, variable.

Questions

Forbidden Words FAQ

Does a team lose a point for saying a forbidden word?

Only if you choose that. The default skips the card with no penalty. You can also choose −1 for the explaining side, or +1 for the team that called it.

Can students play in pairs instead of teams?

Yes. Pair mode takes 2–20 pairs. Partners take turns explaining and guessing, with a pair leaderboard.

How do I choose who comes up?

Before each turn, tap a student's name. The next student in rotation is suggested, so everyone gets a turn.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes: C for correct, P for pass, F for forbidden, Space to pause and Ctrl/⌘+Z to undo.

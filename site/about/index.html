About

Made by a teacher, for the classroom screen

Review Recess makes whole-class review games that are quick to set up, easy to read from the back of the room, and built around your own questions.

Review Recess started in a real classroom. Each game was built by a working teacher who wanted review time to involve the whole class, not just the few students who answer first, and who was tired of rebuilding slide decks for every unit.

What we care about

  • Teacher control. You decide which questions are used, how teams are formed and when to move on.
  • Reuse. A question bank you build once should keep working, term after term.
  • Privacy. The games run in your own Google account. Your questions and class lists stay in your Drive, not ours.
  • Human-written content. Our question packs are written by a teacher and follow the units teachers already teach.

Where to find us

Our games and question packs are sold through Teachers Pay Teachers. For help, visit Help & Support or email our support email (coming soon).

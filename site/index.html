How it works

From purchase to playing in three steps

1

Open your game link

Your TpT download includes the game link. Open it while signed in to Google and allow access once.

2

Choose your questions

Use the sample bank, paste in your own questions, or load a question pack. Untick anything you don't want today.

3

Put it on the big screen

Split the class into teams, go full screen and play. Scores, timers and turns are handled for you.

Why it works in class

Designed around how teachers actually review

Your questions, your choice

A tick-box question picker lets you select all, or read through and untick anything that doesn't fit today's lesson.

Reusable question banks

Save a bank once per unit and load it again next lesson, next term or next year.

Questions never run out

Nothing repeats until every question has been used, then the bank reshuffles on its own.

Whole-class and team play

Team rosters, fair student pickers and rotating turns keep every student involved, not just the fastest hands.

Readable from the back row

Light, dark and high-contrast themes, plus big-text and large-button options for projectors.

Private by design

Games run in your own Google account and only use files they create. Student data never comes to us.

Question packs

Curriculum-aligned packs for the whole year

We're writing review packs that follow the units teachers already teach, so you can use them after every unit, not just before exams. Every question is written by a teacher. First packs in development:

  • AP® World History: Modern
  • AP® United States Government and Politics
  • Grade 8 U.S. History & Civics
  • ELA skills: grammar, vocabulary and more

Packs will appear in the TpT store as each one is finished.

Live mode

Policies

Privacy

Review Recess games run in the teacher's own Google account. Students don't sign in and don't have accounts. We don't collect, see or store any student information, and this website doesn't track you.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

"Review Recess", "we" and "us" mean Review Recess. "You" means the teacher or other adult using our website or games.

1. This website (reviewrecess.com)

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't set any cookies.
  • The site only loads its own files. It doesn't load fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies.
  • There are no sign-ups, accounts or forms on this site.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles basic technical information (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Links to Teachers Pay Teachers (TpT) take you to TpT's website, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How the games work

Each Review Recess game is a Google Apps Script web app. It opens in your web browser from a Google link. There are three kinds.

Free editions and topic packs (no sign-in)

  • These run without anyone signing in. They use no Google permissions at all.
  • Each one plays a built-in question list. You can't save anything to our side, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us or stored on a server.
  • You may type team names to play. Those stay on your screen and in your browser (see "Your browser" below).
  • Because the games are hosted by Google, Google handles the page request under its own policies. We don't receive your name, email address or other details from Google when you open these games.

Editable (full) versions (Google sign-in)

  • These run under your Google account. The first time you open one, Google asks you to approve one permission: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file).
  • With that permission, the game creates one spreadsheet in your own Google Drive for each game (for example, "Million Point Climb – Question Library"). Your question banks and class rosters are saved there.
  • The game remembers which spreadsheet is yours by saving its ID in your own Google account's private script storage (Apps Script "user properties"). Only the game, running as you, can read it.
  • The game can't see your other Drive files, your email, your contacts or your calendar. It doesn't ask for those permissions.
  • We can't see your spreadsheet or anything in it. It belongs to you, in your Drive. We don't receive a copy, and we can't access it unless you choose to share it with us yourself.

Million Point Climb phone remote

  • If you use the optional phone remote in the editable Million Point Climb, your laptop and phone pass the game's state (such as the current question and scores) through Google's temporary per-user storage (Apps Script user cache) in your Google account.
  • Both devices must be signed in to the same Google account. The data expires after about 20 minutes and is cleared when you stop the remote. We can't see it.

Your browser

  • All of our games save some settings in your browser's local storage on that device, such as display theme, sound, difficulty, which questions you've unticked and which question bank or roster you last used.
  • Some games may also keep the current game (for example, player or team names and scores) so a refresh doesn't lose your place.
  • This stays on that computer. It's never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • On the same computer, Million Point Climb's separate display screen talks to the main screen inside your browser (it doesn't go over the internet).

3. Students and children's privacy

Review Recess games are tools that a teacher runs on a classroom screen.

  • Students don't sign in, create accounts or enter personal information into our games.
  • Review Recess doesn't collect, receive or store personal information from students or children under 13.
  • If a teacher adds student names to a roster, those names are saved only in that teacher's own Google spreadsheet (and possibly that teacher's browser). They never come to us.
  • Tip: use team names, first names or initials rather than full names. Please follow your school's and district's rules about student information.

COPPA: Because we don't collect personal information from children, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act's collection rules don't apply to our games as we run them. Teachers should still follow their school's policies.

FERPA: We don't receive or keep student education records. Any information a teacher adds stays in the teacher's own Google account under the school's or teacher's control.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), contact us and we'll delete it.

4. Google API Services: Limited Use

Review Recess's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain words, the editable games use the drive.file permission only to create, read and update the game's own spreadsheet in your Drive so the game works. We don't:

  • transfer that data to anyone;
  • use it for advertising;
  • sell it;
  • use it to train AI models;
  • let any person read it.

5. Purchases on Teachers Pay Teachers

You buy Review Recess products on TpT, under TpT's privacy policy and terms. TpT handles payment. We never see your card details. TpT doesn't give sellers buyers' email addresses or payment details. We can see the limited order information TpT shares with sellers, such as usernames, and we use it only to run our store and support buyers.

6. If you email us

If you email us at support@reviewrecess.com, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you. We keep emails only as long as needed for support and our records, and we'll delete them if you ask. Please don't send student information by email.

7. Who else is involved (third parties)

  • Google runs the games (Apps Script) and stores your spreadsheet in your Drive under Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and forwards email sent to our address.
  • Teachers Pay Teachers handles purchases.

We don't sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

We don't hold your game data, so you're in control:

  • Delete your game data: delete the game's spreadsheet from your Google Drive (and empty the Trash). If you open the game again later, it will make a fresh spreadsheet with sample questions.
  • Remove the game's access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose the Review Recess game and remove its access.
  • Clear browser settings: clear site data for the game in your browser.
  • Phone remote data expires on its own after about 20 minutes.

9. Security

The free editions and packs can't save anything. The editable games use Google's sign-in and permission screens, and they only touch the one spreadsheet they create. The website is served over HTTPS with security headers that block outside scripts. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account protected with a strong password and 2-Step Verification.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the games or website change. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what data a game uses, we'll update this page before that change goes live.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@reviewrecess.com.

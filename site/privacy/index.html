Policies

Privacy

The short version: this website doesn't track you, and our games don't send us your students' information.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This website

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't set any cookies.
  • We don't load fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies.
  • There are no forms or accounts on this site, so we don't collect personal information here.
  • Like any website host, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to keep the site secure and running.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

The games

  • Each game is a Google Apps Script web app that runs in your own Google account.
  • Your question banks and class rosters are saved in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. They are not sent to us, and we can't see them.
  • The games ask for only one Google permission: access to the files the game itself creates. They can't read your other Drive files, your email or your contacts.
  • Some preferences (such as display theme and which questions you have unticked) are saved in your browser's local storage on that computer. They never leave your device.
  • Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to your Google account.

Tip for teachers: games only need team names or first names. Please follow your school's rules about student information.

Live mode (coming soon)

Live mode is not available yet. When it launches, students will join by scanning a QR code with no login, and nothing will be stored after the session ends. We'll update this page with the full details before live mode goes live.

Teachers Pay Teachers

Purchases are handled by Teachers Pay Teachers under its own privacy policy. We receive the order information TpT shares with sellers, not your payment details.
